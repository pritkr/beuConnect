# BEUConnect Product Roadmap & Vision

To become the top-notch platform for BEU students, focusing on engagement, utility, and analytics, here are the top 10 next big ideas for BEUConnect:

## 1. Attendance Tracker & Forecaster (MVP)
A crucial tool that solves a daily pain point. Allows students to track their classes, log present/absent days, and intelligently foresees how many classes they can bunk while maintaining the strict 75% university requirement.

## 2. Subject-Wise Analytics Dashboard
Visualizing passing/failing ratios, difficulty spikes, and scoring ranges for different subjects using aggregated result data. Helps students know which subjects need the most attention.

## 3. Smart Syllabus Progress Tracker
Transforming the current static syllabus viewer into a gamified, interactive to-do list with a progress bar for each subject to help students pace their semester studies.

## 4. College vs College Leaderboard (Batch Battle)
Aggregating college SGPAs and overall performance to rank BEU colleges competitively, fostering a healthy sense of rivalry and pride.

## 5. Internship & Scholarship Wiki
A dedicated, frequently updated hub for off-campus drives, university-specific opportunities, and major scholarships (NSP, PMS, Reliance) tailored for BEU students.

## 6. Lab & Viva Prep Hub
A standardized repository containing lab manuals, clear experiment explanations, and a community-sourced list of the most frequently asked viva questions.

## 7. Gen PYQ (AI Question Predictor)
Integrating AI to analyze the extensive archive of Previous Year Questions (PYQs) to predict important topics, highlight repeating patterns, or generate realistic mock papers for upcoming exams.

## 8. Student Public Profiles
Shareable links where students can proudly showcase their academic journey, overall rank, merit list standing, and "Result Battle" dominance stats to peers or recruiters.

## 9. Mess Routine & Timetable Sync
Digitizing hostel mess menus and class timetables, potentially with home screen widgets showing the "next class" or "next meal," improving daily quality of life.

## 10. Bulk Result Scraper/Generator
A specialized, gated tool to generate structured result spreadsheets for an entire batch instantly, primarily designed for college administrations, CRs, and data-driven analysis.
