Syllabus, previous-year papers, results, rank, attendance and notices for Bihar
Engineering University students, with the syllabus, papers and notices kept on
the device so most of it works without a connection.

Features:
* Syllabus for every BEU branch and semester, split into topics, with GATE
  papers and a saved list.
* Previous year papers with PDFs and a paper finder.
* Results fetched from BEU, a saved copy, the published result tables, or BEU's
  own result page.
* University rank, merit list and a side-by-side result comparison.
* Attendance tracker, SGPA calculator and predictor, routine and holiday
  calendar.
* Notes, planner, notices and search across the bundled data.
* Published college and branch rosters and CGPA leaders.

Data and privacy:
A registration number is stored on the device and sent only to BEU's result
endpoints to resolve a name and result. No account is required and there are no
ads or analytics. Feedback is sent only when you submit it.

Source code: https://github.com/pritkr/beuConnect (AGPL-3.0-or-later).
Not an official BEU app.
