Unofficial student project. Not affiliated with Bihar Engineering University.

Syllabus
The BEU syllabus by branch and semester, split into subjects and topics, with credits and the official subject codes. The GATE syllabus is included by paper and topic. A subject can be saved, and one search finds a subject, a paper, a college or a notice.

Previous-year papers
BEU question papers by branch, semester and subject, with a paper finder and the questions shown as text. GATE papers keep their official PDFs. A branch's papers can be downloaded from Settings and read offline.

Results
Look up a result with a registration number and a semester. The app asks a result service that reads BEU's result server. BEU now puts a captcha in front of the result, so the app also opens BEU's own result page: solve the captcha once and the result is read back into the app. A fetched result shows the paper rows, SGPA and CGPA, and stays on the phone.

Published tables
Result tables from the published college and branch data, in CGPA order and sortable by SGPA or name.

Rank and merit
University rank and a merit list from those tables, and a side-by-side comparison of two results. A college or branch that has not been published has no rank.

Attendance and routine
Subject-wise attendance with the classes needed to reach a given percentage, JSON import and export for it, the weekly routine, a holiday calendar and an SGPA calculator.

Notices
The BEU notice board with search, announcement posts and the official PDFs.

Notes and rosters
Subject notes, a study planner, and the published college and branch rosters with CGPA and branch leaders.

Offline
The syllabus, the GATE set and the paper catalog are built into the app and open with no connection. Papers, result tables, notes and notices you have opened stay readable offline, and Settings can download every paper for a branch and semester.

No account is required and there are no ads. Usage analytics can be turned off in Settings. Feedback is sent only when you submit it.
